Quick start

1. Tap Select video from phone and choose a file in Android's picker.

2. Tap Find automatic clip suggestions, or add your own timestamps.

3. Tap Edit video to add text, emojis, shapes, caption banners, or your own images. Preview and tap Apply edits. Then tap Generate all clips with edits.

4. Tap Save as… to choose a destination and filename, or Download to save through Chrome. You can also share clips to Google Photos or other supported apps.

5. To publish directly to YouTube, host this app at HTTPS, configure OAuth and connect your channel. Review title, description, visibility and audience.

6. Check the resulting format before publishing. For longer videos and Urdu/English transcription, see the cloud guide.
