AI Clip Studio — Simple User Guide

For Pixel 7, OPPO A6c and Android Chrome. The video editor works without YouTube sign-in. YouTube publishing works after the owner configures the app for Google OAuth and each user connects a channel.

New: Context AI (v6 test feature)

What it does: Selects recommended moments using timed Urdu/English dialogue, visual movement and optional descriptions of actions. A caption model may describe a person laughing, running, arguing or celebrating, but it cannot reliably understand an entire TV storyline. You must review the recommendations.

  1. Open the free cloud analysis notebook in Colab; place a video you have permission to edit in your own Google Drive under AI-Clip-Studio/INPUT.
  2. Run the notebook. It transcribes Urdu/English and, if enabled and free compute is available, describes a limited number of sampled frames. It writes context_analysis.json into AI-Clip-Studio/OUTPUT/<run>/.
  3. Open CLIPIT, import the same video from the phone, then import context_analysis.json into Context AI — Dialogue + Actions.
  4. Tap Create context-aware clip suggestions. Read the reasons, preview the selected scenes, adjust start/end times, and edit automatic captions.
  5. Generate and save the clips or use the YouTube review screen once the account is connected. Nothing publishes without your confirmation.

Faster subtitle-only option: If you already have timed SRT/VTT subtitles, import those instead of running Colab. This understands the text but not video actions.

Still not automatic: Google Colab must be started manually. Its free sessions may disconnect and the image-caption model may not download or run. The current browser website is not a 24/7 AI-processing server.

Make your first Short

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Select video from phone, then pick your file from Gallery, Files or Google Photos if Android offers it.
  3. Tap Find automatic clip suggestions, or enter start and end times under Add custom clip.
  4. Tap Edit video to add English/Urdu text, a caption banner, emoji, image or shape.
  5. Tap Apply edits and choose Save clip or Generate all clips with edits.
  6. Once a clip is ready, tap Download or Save as… to choose a folder, when supported.
  7. For YouTube, tap ▶ Publish to YouTube beside a finished clip. Review the title, description, audience and visibility, then confirm.

Note: The automatic finder detects visual activity/scene changes, not Urdu/English speech. There is an optional separate cloud notebook for speech transcription.

Connect YouTube — first-time setup

The app owner must first publish the app as an HTTPS website and register it with Google's OAuth service. This is only once for the app. The owner and friend may then each connect their own channel.

  1. In the app, expand First-time YouTube connection setup.
  2. Enter the Google OAuth Web client ID that the app owner created, unless it is already configured.
  3. Tap Connect YouTube channel.
  4. Choose the correct Google account, review permissions and approve them.
  5. The app loads your YouTube channel and dashboard. If you change accounts or the session expires, tap Connect again.

Detailed setup instructions are in DEPLOY_AND_CONNECT.md in the ZIP. Google passwords are entered on Google's own page, never in this app.

Publish clips directly

The app adds two YouTube options: ▶ Publish beside a planned cut, which first renders it on the phone; and ▶ Publish to YouTube beside an already-rendered clip. You can also choose an existing finished MP4/WebM from the YouTube Studio section.

The upload dialog requires a title, visibility (Private, Unlisted or Public), the made-for-kids audience setting, and rights confirmation. The initial visibility is Private for safe testing. Tap Confirm upload to send it. Keep Chrome open until upload completes. YouTube may then continue processing the video.

Do not press upload twice if your connection drops: check YouTube Studio for a partially accepted upload first.

Read your dashboard

Subscribers and lifetime channel views come from YouTube channel statistics. Recent uploads likes/comments totals are calculated from the latest accessible uploads; they are not lifetime channel totals.

The activity chart shows views over the selected number of days. Watch minutes, subscribers gained and subscribers per 1,000 views are from YouTube Analytics reports. Some recent days may be missing or delayed.

Recent uploads show views, likes, comments and privacy. Recent comments are read-only in v5. You can tap Open video to reply through YouTube. The app cannot track precisely which individual viewer liked a video and then subscribed.

Editing and file storage

Custom clips: use Start and End times or player position buttons. Edit video: select text, caption bar, emoji, shape or image; adjust position, colors, timing and size. Tap Apply edits to keep changes for rendering. Urdu script is supported in text overlays but font rendering depends on the device.

Save as: can open a system picker in compatible Chrome contexts. Download: asks Chrome to save to Downloads. Share/Photos: opens Android sharing if available. Google Photos may not automatically back up Downloads; enable its backup of the Downloads folder if offered. Remove unnecessary temporary source files only after verifying backups.

Export is real-time in the browser; a 30-second clip can take roughly 30 seconds or more. MP4 is chosen only if Chrome supports recording that format. Otherwise WebM may be created. Very long source videos may overload the OPPO. Keep the phone awake during rendering.

If something isn't working

  • No YouTube connect: make sure you are on the deployed HTTPS app, not the local HTML file. Verify authorized JavaScript origin and OAuth client ID.
  • Google app unverified: during testing the owner must add your Google account to Google Cloud OAuth test users; verification may be needed for wider sharing.
  • Dashboard empty: confirm you're signed into the correct account and enabled both YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API.
  • Upload failed: check internet, token expiration, authorized channel, API quota, OAuth scopes and video file format. Review the channel before retrying.
  • Black clip preview: use v5 with the v4.3 safe-seeking fix, re-import the video, and try a phone-recorded H.264 MP4.
  • Save fails: tap Download and check Chrome Downloads, or use a shorter clip. Some Android browsers do not provide custom folder selection.
  • No audio: check browser-supported recording/audio capture for that format; preview your output before posting.

Permissions and safety

Access tokens live in browser memory only and are not saved to the app's local storage. The OAuth client ID is public and may be saved locally. When you upload, your video goes directly from your device to YouTube's official API endpoint. The v5 app does not operate a private video-storage server.

Only publish video you own or have permission to reuse. Adding watermarks, shapes, commentary, or other overlays does not guarantee that a third-party program is copyright-safe or monetizable.
