Privacy & Permissions

AI Clip Studio is a browser-based video editor. In this v5 frontend, selected source media remains on your device during local editing and rendering. YouTube uploads are transmitted directly from your browser to Google's official YouTube Data API only after you confirm an upload.

When you choose Connect YouTube channel, Google Identity Services asks for permission to upload to YouTube, read your channel data and retrieve analytics. Google handles your account password. The app does not request or store it.

OAuth access tokens are held in memory for the current browser tab and cleared on disconnect or reload. An OAuth client ID (which is public app configuration) may be saved in local storage. Channel stats and comments are displayed to the user inside the browser; this frontend does not send them to a separate app developer server.

Click Disconnect session to revoke your current token. You may also manage third-party access from your Google Account settings. The app does not independently store a history of uploads.

Google/YouTube APIs are subject to Google's terms and privacy policy. Use only media you have rights to publish. Contact the app owner if you need help with your deployment or privacy policy.
